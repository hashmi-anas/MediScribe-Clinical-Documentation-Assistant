Clinical Documentation Framework & Definitions

Overview of generative clinical documentation formats produced by MediScribe AI.

SOAP Note Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan Format
Subjective (S): Chief complaints, patient history & symptoms.
Objective (O): Vitals, physical exam & laboratory test data.
Assessment (A): Primary diagnosis & clinical differential evaluations.
Plan (P): Therapeutic interventions, medications & care steps.
Clinical Note Comprehensive Medical Encounter Documentation

Detailed physician progress notes recording clinical evaluation, symptom timelines, procedural notes, differential diagnostic reasoning, and examination assessments.

Rx (Prescription) Medical Prescription & Pharmacotherapy Directive

Structured prescription order specifying pharmaceutical medication names, precise dosage, administration route (oral/topical/IV), frequency, course duration, and patient safety guidelines.

Discharge Summary Hospitalization & Treatment Closure Summary

Formal clinical discharge document summarizing admission reason, hospital course, key interventions, discharge diagnosis, reconciled medications, and post-discharge recovery instructions.

Follow-up Plan Post-Consultation Care & Monitoring Directives

Scheduled return consultation dates, required interval laboratory/imaging monitoring, rehabilitation milestones, and red-flag warning symptoms requiring immediate clinical evaluation.
