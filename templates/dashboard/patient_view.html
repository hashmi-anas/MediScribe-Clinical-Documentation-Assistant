Medical Documentation Types & Guide

Understand the different clinical notes and reports generated by your doctor during consultations.

SOAP Note Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan Summary
Subjective (S): Your symptoms, feelings & reasons for the visit.
Objective (O): Physical vitals, lab reports & examination findings.
Assessment (A): The doctor's diagnosis and medical assessment.
Plan (P): Prescriptions, tests, treatment steps & health advice.
Clinical Note Complete Visit Record

Detailed notes written by your physician summarizing your symptoms, examination observations, medical history review, and expert clinical conclusions.

Rx (Prescription) Official Doctor's Prescription

The authorized medical prescription stating exact medication names, doses, timing (morning/night), duration, food instructions, and refill allowances.

Discharge Summary Visit & Treatment Summary

A complete summary of your hospital or clinic visit, treatments given, condition upon discharge, home medications, and self-care recommendations.

Follow-up Plan Next Steps & Care Schedule

Schedule of your upcoming doctor appointments, required follow-up blood/lab tests, lifestyle recovery guidance, and red-flag symptoms to watch out for.
