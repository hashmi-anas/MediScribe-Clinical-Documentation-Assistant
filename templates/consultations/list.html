Clinical Documentation Framework & Definitions

Overview of documentation formats generated for patient consultations.

SOAP Note Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan Format
Subjective (S): Chief complaint, history & symptoms.
Objective (O): Physical examination, vitals & labs.
Assessment (A): Primary diagnosis & clinical differentials.
Plan (P): Therapeutic interventions & management.
Clinical Note Comprehensive Medical Encounter Documentation

Detailed physician notes recording clinical examination, timeline of symptoms, procedural notes, differential diagnostic reasoning, and observations.

Rx (Prescription) Medical Prescription & Pharmacotherapy Directive

Structured prescription specifying pharmaceutical medication names, precise dosage, route (oral/topical/IV), frequency, course duration, and patient safety guidance.

Discharge Summary Hospitalization & Treatment Closure Summary

Formal clinical discharge summary documenting admission reason, hospital course, key interventions, discharge diagnosis, reconciled medications, and home care instructions.

Follow-up Plan Post-Consultation Care & Monitoring Directives

Scheduled return consultation dates, required interval laboratory/imaging monitoring, rehabilitation milestones, and red-flag warning symptoms.
